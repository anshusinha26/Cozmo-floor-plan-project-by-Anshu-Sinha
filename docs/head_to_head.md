# Head to head against AR Plan 3D

AR Plan 3D by Grymala on Android. Protocol: one casual attempt per room, corners hidden by furniture estimated by the operator.

Every dimension is compared against the tape reading, which is the only thing either side can be right or wrong about. A tie means the two errors differ by less than 1.5 cm.

Not compared: magicplan. Offers no camera scan on Android, so there is nothing to compare on the same device. Screenshot kept in data/own/app_comparision.
